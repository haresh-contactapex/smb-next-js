Our Story

Custom wedding bands, made by our own jewelers

shopmyband.com is a division of My Bridal Ring focused on custom wedding bands for men and women. My Bridal Ring is a manufacturer based in Los Angeles area for over 25 years. It is family owned and operated with over 60 years of combined experience from diamond manufacturing from rough diamonds to entire process of jewelry manufacturing. My Bridal Ring consist of individuals who have passion for diamond jewelry who have come together with a simple mission – to bring quality and experience to people buying an engagement ring online, rather than in person.

The most important benefit to purchasing online is the dramatically reduced cost to the customers. Because we are direct manufacturer, our jewelers are able to eliminate the overhead of operating a store, and can pass on those savings to customers.

We offer personalized, free engraving and free lifetime service and cleaning.
