Jewelry is available in a variety of metals. There are three basic metals used in jewelry, namely – silver, gold, and platinum.

Jewelry Care

Buying jewelry means a great deal of investment. And now that you are the proud owner of that piece of jewelry you had set your heart on, you should take proper care of it so that it maintain its shine and beauty for years to come.

Regardless of whether it is a bracelet, a ring, or a pair of earrings, you should get your jewelry professionally cleaned and inspected at regular intervals. However, these professional sessions are not enough. You also need to clean the jewelry at home on a regular basis.

Most pieces can be cleaned with simple soap and water. You can also use a soft brush to reach the corners and remove any dust and grime.

Professional diamond cleaning kits are also available. These kits are especially made to clean diamond jewelry. Similarly, silver polish is available to clean your silver jewelry and remove any tarnish.

Some key points that you can keep in mind to take care of your jewelry is to avoid wearing your jewelry while exercising. This is because you tend to perspire while exercising and this can diminish the shine of the jewelry. Also, don’t expose your jewelry to perfume, or any harsh chemicals used in household cleaning. Ensure that you store your jewelry in a soft pouch or small box so that it is not exposed to air.

Keeping these few points in mind, you can go ahead and shop for the special piece of jewelry you had your eye on since long.
