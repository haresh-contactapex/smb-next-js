Wedding bands and eternity bands both represent love and long-lasting relationship between two people. They reinforce the wedding ritual and stand as a forever memory for the couple. However, they are different when it comes to design, purpose, and meaning. Generally, wedding bands are traditional and minimalistic in design whereas eternity bands feature a grand show of diamonds and gemstones.

Through this article, we shall talk about eternity bands and wedding bands in detail, outlining the key differences between the two.

Let’s start with the definitions.

What are eternity bands?

Eternity Wedding Bands are designed with a continuous line of diamonds or gemstones encircling the ring. Couples present diamond eternity bands on special occasions like milestone anniversaries as a token of love and gratitude for each other. Some people also use these rings as wedding bands. At Shop My Band, you will find full eternity and half eternity bands. In the former, the gems are arranged all around the ring while stones envelop the top half in half eternity bands.

What are wedding bands?

Wedding bands are simple rings prepared from smooth metal like gold or platinum. They reflect the purity of marriage. They are subtly designed and often paired with an engagement ring. Nowadays, modern metals like Tungsten are also used for Wedding Bands.

Now that you are familiar with what these rings are, we will throw light on detailed differences in terms of design, aesthetics, price, comfort, versatility, and much more.

Eternity Bands Versus Wedding Bands

1. Design and Look

Eternity bands and wedding bands are considerably different when it comes to their design and aesthetics. While eternity bands are intricately designed, wedding bands are minimal and complement an engagement ring. Eternity bands often feature gemstones continuously arranged in the form of infinity. Wedding bands, on the other hand, have small accent diamonds and the design focuses on the metal instead of gemstones.

2. Symbolization

Eternity band, as the name suggests, epitomizes eternal love. The arrangement of diamonds in a continuous line showcases a long-lasting bond that lasts forever. Wedding band also stands for love and relationship but it is more about the purity of marriage.

3. Occasion

As a tradition, couples exchange wedding bands during the marriage ceremony. The ritual stands for the vows taken between the two people. Contrarily, eternity bands are given on special wedding anniversaries or milestones in line. These bands serve as a gift that commemorates how far they have come together. 4. Comfort

Wedding bands are more comfortable owing to their smooth and sleek design. You can wear them every day without any discomfort. Eternity bands, particularly the ones with large diamonds are heavier and can snag on your clothes.

5. Versatility

You can pair wedding bands with different types of engagement rings. They can be worn daily without causing any hindrance. Eternity bands can be too gaudy to wear every day. This is particularly true if you already have a lavish engagement ring on your finger.

6. Affordability

Traditional wedding bands are more affordable than eternity bands. Eternity bands are priced according to the number of stones and their size. If you use Lab-Grown Diamonds, it would be less expensive compared to traditionally mined diamonds. Wedding bands with minimal gemstones and plain metal are low cost, making them light on the pocket and popular among the masses.
