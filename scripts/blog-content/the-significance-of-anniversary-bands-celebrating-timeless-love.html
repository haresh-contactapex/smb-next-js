How did Anniversary Bands Originate?

If we go back in history, husbands in ancient Rome used to give their wives a silver wreath called ‘annulus pronubus’ on their 25th anniversary. With time, different milestone anniversaries started to be associated with different materials.

In the Victorian era, jewelry was used to mark anniversaries. Gold along with gemstones like pearls and diamonds stood for eternity and purity. Rings were engraved with cheesy messages or important dates.

Importance of Different Anniversary Milestones

The 10th anniversary is also known as the tin anniversary. It symbolizes the strength and durability of a couple’s relationship just like tin is renowned for its corrosion resistance.

The 25th anniversary is known as the silver anniversary. It symbolizes a quarter of a century spent together. Silver stands for purity and clarity.

The 50th anniversary is considered to be the golden anniversary. Gold is a priceless metal that stands for wealth, prosperity, and wisdom. It depicts the huge treasure of memories they have made as a couple.
