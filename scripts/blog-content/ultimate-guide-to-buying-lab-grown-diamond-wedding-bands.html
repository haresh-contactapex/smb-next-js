A wedding band serves as a testimony to a couple’s bond and the love they share. Purchasing a wedding band is one of the most remarkable moments for the bride and groom. In the recent times, people are opting for lab-grown diamond wedding bands. This growing preference shift can be attributed to their affordability without compromising on the beauty of real diamonds.

What are lab grown diamond bands?

It’s no longer a distant dream as man-made diamonds are used to manufacture lab created diamond bands in gemological labs. The workers study the process of creation of an original diamond and emulate it in the lab. The lab grown diamonds possess the same physical and chemical structure, like original diamonds.

Usually, a wedding band has small gems distributed evenly throughout the ring, bonded tightly, unlike engagement rings where there’s a single gem as the centrepiece. The design of wedding bands depicts unity and partnership between two people.

That being said, let’s understand the process of preparing lab-grown diamond wedding band.

Manufacturing Lab-Grown Diamond Bands

There are two widely used techniques to prepare lab-grown diamond bands:

a. HPHT: High-pressure High-temperature technique

b. CVD: Chemical Vapor Deposition

Once the stone is created, the professional craftsman puts it carefully onto a band.

Salient Features of Lab-Grown Diamond Bands

As lab grown wedding band is created in a controlled setup comparable to the Earth’s natural diamond-forming processes, they have several remarkable features listed below:

1. Certified Excellence

GIA and IGI evaluate the lab grown diamonds, considering the 4 Cs, namely cut, color, clarity, and carat.

2. Adherence to Ethical Regulations

These diamond bands have transparent supply chains with zero disputes.

3. Environment-friendly

In comparison to traditional mining, lab grown diamond bands have minimum ecological footprint.

4. Superior Value

They are generally easier to access when compared to mined diamonds of same quality.
