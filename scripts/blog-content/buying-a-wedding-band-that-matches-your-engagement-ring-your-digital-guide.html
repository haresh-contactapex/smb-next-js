Purchasing an ideal engagement ring is an extremely time-consuming task, considering the variety of shapes, designs, and metals you get nowadays. What is even tougher is buying a complementary wedding ring to go with it. While purchasing the wedding ring, you need to make sure that it does not repress the engagement ring but brings the best out of it.

Through this blog, we shall discuss how you can buy complementary wedding bands that make you shine brighter.

Metal type

The wedding band you like and the engagement ring should have the same metal type. Here, the metal type also refers to the carats in the band. For example, if you are wearing a 22 carat gold engagement ring, make sure your wedding band follows the same trend. If you have planned for rose gold wedding bands, you must invest in rose gold engagement rings too. The same applies to platinum wedding bands and all other types of bands. Consistency is the most important factor while shopping for complementary bridal jewelry.

Curved wedding bands

Traditional diamond wedding bands would not go well with engagement rings having a large centre stone. In such cases, notch or curved wedding rings are the best bet. As these rings come with a little bend or notch in the band, it can accommodate the diamond in the engagement ring pretty well. The wedding band, therefore, houses perfectly with the engagement ring. Diamond Embellishment

In the olden times, men’s and women’s wedding bands used to be simple without any gemstones or diamonds. However, modern couples like to go for diamond embellishment. This also applies to men’s wedding bands. Diamonds are no longer just girl’s best friend. Guys are equally fond of them. The only thing to be checked is the diamond size. Your engagement ring and wedding band should have diamonds of a similar size, shape, and quality so that the jewelry does not look out of place.

Complementing Colored Stone Engagement Rings

In case your engagement rings are bejeweled with ruby or emerald gemstones, you can go for diamond wedding bands to complement these vibrant jewelry pieces.
