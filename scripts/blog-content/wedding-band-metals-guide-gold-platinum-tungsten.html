Whenever you are looking forward to buying a wedding band, it is of utmost importance to consider the metal type in addition to the design. The type of metal ascertains the durability, maintenance, look, and feel of the wedding band. It also uplifts the beauty of your engagement ring. You get wedding bands in different types of metals like gold, tungsten, and platinum.

Through this article, we shall explore how various metal types influence wedding bands so that you can make the right buying decision.

1. Gold

As far as engagement rings or wedding bands are concerned, gold is an ageless metal. Its splendor and versatility make it one of the most admired metals. Shop My Band has different types of gold wedding bands in different karats. While 14K gold imparts more durability for routine wear, 18K bands are purer in quality.

It is available in three main shades, namely:

a. Yellow Gold

People love yellow gold jewelry for the traditional look. With a rich, warm hue, it is the best pick for people who like vintage vibes.

b. White Gold

White gold contains metals like palladium or nickel. Its rhodium coating offers additional sparkle and a modern look.

c. Rose Gold

Rose gold wedding bands have a subtle pink hue that goes well with diamonds. It imparts all the romantic feelings for a couple.

2. Tungsten

As one of the hardest metals, Tungsten offers unparalleled scratch resistance to any jewelry piece. Tungsten wedding bands are available in different colors like black, gray, and white. However, you need to be very careful with the sizing of Tungsten men’s and women’s wedding bands as they cannot be resized.

3. Platinum

Platinum wedding bands come in a natural white color. Its features include durability, resistance to tarnish and wear, and hypoallergenic properties. Despite being more expensive than gold, its premium and sophisticated look makes it a valuable possession for any bride or groom.
