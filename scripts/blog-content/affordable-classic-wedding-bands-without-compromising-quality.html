Type of Metal

Sterling silver metal looks like white gold and is available at a much lower price. Couples looking for classic gold wedding band at a budget can consider this alternative or go for gold plated wedding bands. Modern couples also go for Tungsten and Cobalt wedding bands as they are affordable and durable. Sleek designs are quite popular in Classic Men’s Wedding Bands.

Type of diamonds

Ladies who love diamonds can go for lab grown diamonds instead of traditionally mined ones. It will help you buy budget friendly Classic Women’s Wedding Bands without settling for less.

Size of diamonds/gemstones

You can reduce the size of diamonds or gemstones to be studded on your ring. It will reduce a major chunk of the ring cost.

When to buy

Keep an eye on different offers rolled out by the retailers or online stores. For example, some stores have a sale after major holidays like Valentine’s Day or Christmas. You can also check Black Friday deals at jewelry stores.

Customizations

You can customize the classic wedding band by reducing the width of the band, size of diamonds/stones, and type of gemstones. Certain gemstones are more expensive. So, if possible, avoid those options.
