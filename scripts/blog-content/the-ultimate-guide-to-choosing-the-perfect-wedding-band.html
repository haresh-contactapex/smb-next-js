Your wedding band is more than just a piece of jewelry – it's a symbol of your love and commitment that you'll wear every day. Choosing the perfect ring can feel overwhelming and the options are endless - white gold, yellow gold or platinum wedding bands? Should you choose matching mens and womens wedding bands? Are rose gold wedding bands in vogue?

We want to understand your questions – that’s why, we’ve put together a handy guide to ensure you have all the information you need to find the ideal band to celebrate your union.

Consider your lifestyle

Before diving into styles, think about your daily activities. If you work with your hands or lead an active lifestyle, opt for a durable metal like platinum. For those with sensitive skin, hypoallergenic options like gold (14k or higher) or platinum wedding bands are best.

Choose a metal

Popular choices include:

  • Gold (yellow, white, or rose)
  • Platinum
  • Palladium
  • Titanium
  • Tungsten

Each metal has its pros and cons in terms of durability, cost, and appearance. Research your options to find a piece that aligns with your preferences and budget.

Select a style

Wedding bands come in various styles:

  • Classic: These simple, timeless bands are an evergreen choice for your wedding day
  • Vintage: Intricate designs with a nod to the past, a vintage band is a great choice for a bride with old worldly charm.
  • Modern: The modern bride and groom often favors sleek, contemporary wedding bands
  • Eternity: These wedding bands set with continuous stones are a symbol of love meant to endure.
  • Mixed metal: Combining two or more metals

Consider your personal taste and whether you want your ring to match your engagement ring (if applicable).

Decide on embellishments

Do you want a plain band or something with more sparkle? Diamond wedding bands or rings with gemstones are popular. Other options include engravings, with patterns or personal messages and textured finishes such as hammered, brushed, and more.

Remember, added embellishments can increase the cost and maintenance of your ring.

Get the right fit

Proper sizing is essential for both men’s and women’s wedding bands. Get professionally measured and consider how your finger size might fluctuate with temperature or weight changes. Some styles, like comfort-fit bands, may feel better for everyday wear.

Set a budget

Wedding rings vary widely in price. Decide your budget early on to narrow down on options. Remember, a higher price doesn't always mean better quality or suitability for your needs. Explore a range of affordably priced white, yellow and rose gold wedding bands at Shop My Band.

Choosing your wedding ring should be an enjoyable experience. Do your research, take your time, and trust your instincts. With careful consideration, you'll find a ring that you'll love wearing for years to come.
