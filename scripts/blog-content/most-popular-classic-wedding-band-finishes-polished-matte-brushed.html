Top 3 Finishes for Classic Wedding Bands

Polished

Polished finish renders a shiny, mirror-like appearance to the wedding bands. Light reflects from these surfaces to enhance the ornament’s beauty. Careful buffing and polishing eliminates any irregularities on the metal surface and gives a shiny appearance to the jewelry. You get polished classic gold wedding band at Shop My Band website. Note that polished finish is bound to gather some scratches. If you like your wedding bands as shiny as new, you should get them repolished at regular intervals.

Polished wedding bands are perfect for people who want to bring together tradition and sophistication. They are versatile enough to go with any type of metal.

Matte

Matte wedding bands have a velvety finish without any shine. For couples who are looking forward to wearing the jewelry everyday, matte classic wedding bands are the safest bet. As these bands have rougher surfaces unlike polished wedding bands, they are not so susceptible to minor scratches. Matte wedding bands are best for platinum, gold, and titanium wedding bands.

Brushed

Brushed finish is a hybrid of matte and polished finishes. As it features lines on the surface, it looks like a matte finish that does not reflect light. Brushed finish is extremely popular in classic men’s wedding bands. You can select this finish for any metal type.

Miscellaneous Finishes

Apart from the top 3 finishes shared above, there are some other finishes that are getting immense momentum these days. One of them is hammered finish. These wedding bands are designed by bending the surface of the wedding band with a hammer so that it reflects light. Couples looking for something unique and artistic should go for this style. Another type is satin finish. It gives little shine to the wedding band. If you are looking for something subtle without any extra shimmer, go for satin finish.
