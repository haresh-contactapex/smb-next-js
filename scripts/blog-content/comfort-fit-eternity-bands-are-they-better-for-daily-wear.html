Defining Comfort Fit Eternity Band

A comfort fit ring is designed with a rounded interior rather than a flat one. Hence, it glides more easily over the knuckle and sits more naturally on the finger. It not only reduces pressure points but also reduces friction against the skin. It feels slightly looser without being loose.

Unlike plain metal bands, eternity rings have stones between fingers. As a result, it can create pressure points during everyday tasks. Therefore, comfort fit becomes a necessity.
