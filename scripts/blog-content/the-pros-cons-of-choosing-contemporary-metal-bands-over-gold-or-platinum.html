Let’s start with gold, the timeless choice for everyone.

Pros and cons of gold wedding bands

These metal bands are available as white, yellow, and rose gold. It is the most versatile metal and favorite among the masses.

Pros

Gold is available in diverse designs. You can choose the one according to your budget. Gold wedding bands are easy to resize and repair.

Cons

Gold jewelry scratches more easily. It is softer than platinum and tungsten. If you go for white gold, you should be prepared for regular maintenance like rhodium re-plating. 22K or 24K gold might not be suitable for routine use.

Pros and cons of platinum wedding bands

Platinum is perfect for guys looking for sophistication and durability.

Pros

It is a naturally white metal. So, it does not require any plating to be done. As it is a dense metal, you can vouch for its durability. Men with sensitive skin can go for platinum wedding bands as it is hypoallergenic. Shop My Band has a wide range of platinum wedding bands.

Cons

Platinum wedding bands are priced on the higher side when compared to gold and tungsten. They can scratch but do not lead to metal loss. They feel somewhat heavy on the fingers.

Pros and cons of tungsten wedding bands

Tungsten carbide is the most suitable alternative for men seeking durability and affordability.

Pros

Tungsten is a scratch-resistant and extremely durable metal. It is affordable and available in various finishes like matte, polished, and black Tungsten.

Cons

You cannot resize Tungsten wedding bands. They can shatter or crack under extreme force.

Pros and cons of titanium wedding band

Titanium is a lightweight metal that makes it popular among many people.

Pros

Affordability is the key advantage of men’s titanium wedding band. It is unbreakable, scratch-resistant, and durable. It is easy to clean and requires very little maintenance.

Cons

You cannot easily find titanium rings. As far as variety is concerned, titanium wedding bands cannot be customized to fancy designs. They cannot be resized, once the shape is cut.

Pros and cons of cobalt wedding band

Cobalt is a great choice for couples looking for a bright white metal ring studded with diamonds and other gemstones.

Pros

Cobalt resembles white gold in many ways. It has the upper hand when compared to gold as it doesn’t require any rhodium plating. You can also plate cobalt to resemble yellow or rose gold. Cobalt wedding bands are resistant to tarnish or rust and require minimal maintenance.

Cons

Cobalt rings are hard to resize. They hold no investment value. You won’t find too many designs in cobalt wedding bands.

Wrap Up

Every metal is unique and which one to choose depends on your preferences, skin type, and the kind of look and feel you are looking for. What’s important is the love and respect you have in the relationship. A pretty wedding band is just a bonus. Make an informed decision with all the factors discussed here and carry it with a confident smile. That would make you look all the more beautiful.
